## THE AUDIENCE FIRST PROTOCOL

Stop creating for yourself. Start creating for the person watching.

# YOUR VIDEO IS NOT ABOUT YOU. IT'S ABOUT THEM.

Before you record a video, ask yourself one question:

**Why would someone care about what I'm about to show them?**

People don't open TikTok, YouTube, or Instagram hoping to watch another advertisement. They're looking for entertainment, solutions, inspiration, information, discoveries, or something that speaks to their interests.

Your job as a creator is to connect what you're presenting with what your audience actually cares about.

## THE PSYCHOLOGY OF GETTING PEOPLE TO CARE

**1. Understand their world.**

What frustrates them? What do they wish were easier? What are they curious about? What problem are they trying to solve?

**2. Make the video relevant.**

Don't just introduce a product. Show why it matters to the person watching.

**3. Sell the outcome, not just the object.**

People want to understand what a product can do for them.

Don't simply show a cleaning product. Show how it can make cleaning easier.

Don't simply show skincare. Explain who it may benefit and what it is designed to do.

Don't simply show a gadget. Demonstrate the problem it helps solve.

**4. Create an emotional connection.**

Use relatable situations, storytelling, curiosity, humor, surprise, and honest demonstrations.

Make viewers feel that you understand what they're looking for.

**5. Give them a reason to act.**

Explain the value. Answer their questions. Address their doubts. Let them make an informed decision.

## THE ULTRA VESTRA RULE

Your audience is the reason your video exists.

If your content doesn't connect with their interests, needs, questions, or desires, you may be creating a video that only makes sense to you.

**Create with them in mind. Speak their language. Show them the value.**

Don't interrupt their scrolling with another advertisement.

Give them a reason to stop, watch, understand, and care.

## The futuristic learning experience: How audience psychology works

The four-stage psychological journey:

* ATTENTION — Make me stop.

    The opening must connect to something the audience recognizes, wants, fears missing out on, or finds interesting.

* RELEVANCE — This is for me.

    Make the viewer recognize their own situation in the video.

* DESIRE — I can see the value.

    Demonstrate how the product or information could improve their situation.

* ACTION — I know what to do next.

    Give them a clear, honest reason to click, learn more, or purchase.

## Example: Same product, completely different psychology

Creator-centered video:

"Look what I bought! This serum is amazing."

Audience-centered video:

"Want to simplify your skincare routine? Here's what this serum is designed to do and how I use it."

The second example gives the viewer a reason to care before asking them to care about the product.

## The Audience Alignment Test (the UltraVestra challenge)

Before publishing, make sure your video has a purpose for the person watching.

1. Who am I creating this video for?

Define the audience.

2. What does this audience care about?

Identify an interest, need, or problem.

3. Why would they stop scrolling?

Find the most relevant opening.

4. What value will they receive?

Show the benefit or useful information.

5. Why should they trust me?

Use evidence, honesty, and demonstration.
