# The Two Pillar Systems — Definitions (kept distinct)

UltraVestra uses the word "pillars" for TWO different things. They
are not versions of each other, neither replaces the other, and they
must never be merged or cross-numbered. This document is the
standing definition (Phase 1 Build 4, per the Phase Zero audit's
recommendation: keep both, with distinct roles).

## The Seven Product Pillars — what the product DOES

The architecture of the offering: the seven capability areas of the
Creator Business Operating System, from the 20/10 Master Product
Strategy. They organize what gets built and why. They are **not**
navigation and never appear as a numbered list in the app.

1. **Creator Command Center** — the creator's home base and overview.
2. **Content & Growth Studio** — planning, tracking, improving content.
3. **Opportunity Marketplace** — brand discovery and matching (staged:
   directory → matching → verified campaigns → brand side).
4. **Creator Business Management** — proposals, deals, deliverables,
   the working pipeline of a creator's business.
5. **Creator Finance** — recorded revenue, estimates kept separate,
   business health.
6. **Creator Intelligence** — the three Brains (Video, Brand,
   Performance): understand, explain, identify, recommend, assist.
7. **Creator Academy** — education that teaches the business, tied to
   the tools.

## The Nine Galactic Pillars — the worlds the creator SEES

The experience map shown on `/pillars`: nine named worlds that
organize the platform's tools around the creator's journey. This is
product surface — copy, cards, navigation aids — not architecture.

01 Brand Galaxy · 02 Content Lab · 03 The Money · 04 Growth Engine ·
05 Creator Intelligence · 06 Knowledge Vault · 07 Campaign Match ·
08 Protection (a principle pillar — it has no tool buttons; it names
a commitment, and its panel honestly says what is not yet available)
· 09 Intelligence & Automation.

(The Nine grew from the earlier Six Worlds reorganization; worlds
and pillars in this set are the same list.)

## The rule that keeps them distinct

- A **Seven** item answers "what capability is this and why does it
  exist?" — used in strategy, planning, and build documents.
- A **Nine** item answers "where does the creator find this?" — used
  in the app, on `/pillars`, and in creator-facing copy.
- One capability can appear in both systems under different names
  (e.g. Seven #6 Creator Intelligence ↔ Nine 05 Creator
  Intelligence) — that overlap is expected and is not a conflict to
  resolve.
- The Strategic Charter's **Ten Divisions** are a third, separate
  scheme (company organization). They complement the Nine Pillars
  and never replace them; they are not part of either list above.
